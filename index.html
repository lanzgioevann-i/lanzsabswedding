Our Story

Every love story is beautiful, but ours is our favorite.

How We Met

Their story began at work.

The Bride and the Groom were both part of the same project—but miles apart. The Bride was based in Cebu as a QA Tester, while the Groom was in Manila working as a Developer. Although they worked together, neither of them imagined that a simple typo would change everything.

One day, while sending a message on Microsoft Teams, the Bride meant to type “Kuya Lanz” Instead, she accidentally typed “Kuya Labz”

That one little typo sparked a conversation, followed by laughter, friendship, and eventually something much more. What started as an innocent mistake became the beginning of their greatest love story.

Who knew that one misplaced letter would lead them here—celebrating forever together?

The Proposal

The Bride had always dreamed of going on her first international trip, and the Groom—being the loving and supportive partner he has always been—said yes without hesitation. (As always!)

Their first adventure together was a trip to Hong Kong, booked during the first quarter of 2024. Little did the Bride know, the Groom wasn't just planning a vacation—he was planning one of the biggest moments of their lives.

For months, the Groom carefully kept his plan a secret, making sure the Bride wouldn't suspect a thing.

Then came October 15, 2024.

While the Bride was simply enjoying the trip of a lifetime, the Groom was quietly trying to keep his composure. Behind every smile was a racing heart, and behind every moment was one life-changing question he couldn't wait to ask.

With a very nervous heart and a ring in his hand, the Groom got down on one knee and asked the Bride to spend forever with him.

The answer was “Yes!”

What the Bride thought would be her first international adventure became the beginning of their greatest adventure yet—a lifetime of love, laughter, and forever together.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is parking available?

Yes! Parking is available at both the preparation venue and the reception venue. The two locations are also within walking distance of each other.

What are the check-in and check-out times at GC's Farmhouse Resort?

Check-in is 2:00 PM on February 26, 2027, and check-out is 12:00 PM on February 28, 2027.

Will food be provided?

Yes! We'll be providing meals throughout your 3-day stay, including all meals on our wedding day. If you have specific cravings or dietary preferences beyond what's being served, you're welcome to order from Soriano's Restaurant or any available online food delivery service.

Where will everyone be staying?

All guests will be staying at GC's Farmhouse Resort. We kindly ask everyone to help us keep the resort clean and treat it with care throughout the stay.

Will transportation be provided?

Yes, a van will be available on the wedding day. Priority will be given to our family and friends traveling from Cebu. If there are available seats, please feel free to coordinate with our wedding coordinator.

Will there be a program during the reception?

We're keeping things simple and intimate, so there won't be a formal reception program. We hope you'll simply relax, enjoy the food, celebrate with us, and make wonderful memories together.

Why is the wedding so intimate?

We've chosen to celebrate our special day with a small gathering of 40 guests or fewer, surrounded by our closest family and friends. While we wish we could celebrate with everyone, keeping our wedding intimate allows us to spend meaningful time with each of the people who mean the most to us. Thank you for understanding and for being part of our love story.

Will Mallow be at the wedding?

Yes! Our little daughter, Mallow, will be joining us. She might have her playful (or dramatic 😄) moments, so thank you in advance for your patience and understanding!

We can't wait to celebrate with you.
